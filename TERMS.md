# City Cleanup Challenge Terms of Service

Effective date: July 26, 2026

By using City Cleanup Challenge, you agree to provide accurate account
information, submit only cleanup work you are authorized to document, and
follow local safety and waste-disposal rules.

## Eligible use

- Submit original before-and-after evidence of non-hazardous solid-waste
  cleanup.
- Do not submit AI-generated, manipulated, duplicate, stolen, or previously
  rewarded evidence.
- Do not expose faces, private addresses, vehicle plates, or another person's
  personal data unnecessarily.
- Do not handle medical, biological, chemical, explosive, or other hazardous
  waste through this program.
- Do not interfere with verification, accounts, wallets, infrastructure, or
  reward controls.

## Accounts and moderation

You are responsible for your account and linked wallet. Never provide a seed
phrase or private key to the service. Evidence may be automatically checked and
manually reviewed. The service may reject evidence, reverse an unpaid decision,
pause rewards, restrict an account, or preserve records needed to investigate
abuse. Available appeals follow the published product rules.

## Rewards

CELO rewards are promotional, conditional, and not guaranteed. Pilot amounts,
eligibility, limits, and availability may change prospectively. Testnet CELO
has no promised monetary value. A reward is complete only after the recorded
transaction receives the required confirmations. Public blockchain records are
permanent and controlled by the relevant network, not by this application.

## Safety and availability

Participate only where cleanup is lawful and safe. The service does not direct
users to enter dangerous or private property and does not provide environmental,
medical, legal, tax, or financial advice. The service may be changed, suspended,
or discontinued and is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted
availability.

## Privacy, support, and governing review

Use of personal information is described in [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md).
Support and complaint channels are in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md). Before public
launch, the project owner must add the responsible legal entity, governing law,
minimum-age rule, jurisdiction-specific consumer terms, and an enforceable
contact address.
