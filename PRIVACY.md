# City Cleanup Challenge Privacy Notice

Effective date: July 26, 2026

City Cleanup Challenge processes account details, cleanup photos, approximate
or precise location, device-provided capture metadata, verification results,
moderation history, and Celo wallet and transaction information to operate the
cleanup evidence and reward service.

## Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, show nearby cleanup activity,
verify that evidence is authentic and unique, prevent duplicate rewards and
fraud, support moderation and appeals, make approved Celo payments, secure the
service, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations.

Location is requested only when a location-dependent action is used. Denying
location may prevent GPS verification or event check-in but must not prevent a
user from reading this notice or managing their account.

## Sharing and public visibility

Evidence photos, precise GPS coordinates, verification signals, email addresses,
phone numbers, and authentication data are private. Authorized reviewers and
operators may access them for the purposes above. Service providers may process
data under restricted instructions for hosting, storage, monitoring, email, or
security. Only claim identifiers, verified wallet addresses, reward amounts,
and transaction data intended for Celo are public on-chain.

Do not submit faces, vehicle plates, private addresses, hazardous material, or
another person's personal information unless an approved safety process
requires it. Blockchain transactions cannot be erased.

## Retention and deletion

The approved retention schedule is defined in
[`docs/PRIVACY_RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md`](docs/PRIVACY_RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md).
Users may request access, correction, export, or account deletion through the
support channel in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md). Identity must be verified before
account information is disclosed or deleted.

Deletion removes or anonymizes account profile data and private evidence when
retention is no longer required. Minimal fraud-prevention, security, audit,
financial, and on-chain records may be retained for their documented periods.

## Security and changes

The service uses access controls, private object storage, encryption in transit,
secret management, audit events, and operational monitoring. No system can
guarantee absolute security. Material changes to this notice require a new
effective date and release note.

This notice must be reviewed for the laws of every launch jurisdiction before a
public release.
