# Support and Production Operations

Until a real support mailbox is configured, use the repository's GitHub issue
tracker for non-sensitive product and technical reports. Never post passwords,
tokens, private keys, precise GPS coordinates, private evidence photos, or
identity documents in an issue.

Security incidents and privacy or deletion requests require the private support
channel configured for the deployed environment. Public deployment is blocked
until that monitored address, named owner, backup owner, and service hours are
recorded in the launch ticket and published in the application.

Target response times:

- active security, payout, or personal-data incident: acknowledge within 30
  minutes;
- account compromise or destructive-action report: acknowledge within 4 hours;
- privacy, export, correction, or deletion request: acknowledge within 3
  business days;
- moderation appeal or ordinary product support: acknowledge within 5 business
  days.

Operators follow [`docs/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`](docs/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md) and
[`docs/PRIVACY_RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md`](docs/PRIVACY_RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md).
